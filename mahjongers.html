Mahjongers privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Mahjongers is a riichi mahjong game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your information to the developer or to any server.

What stays on your device

The app saves your settings, the name and character you choose, your game history and progress, and any game in progress. This is stored only on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Playing with friends

“Play with friends” connects phones that are near each other directly over your local network (Wi‑Fi or peer‑to‑peer), so it works without the internet. iOS asks your permission before the app uses the local network.

When you join a table, the other phones at that table receive the name and character you chose, a random ID the app made so you get your seat back if you drop out, and the moves of the game. Nothing is sent beyond the phones at that table.

TestFlight

If you test a beta through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, device information and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. Apple’s handling of that data is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions? Send feedback through TestFlight, or open an issue at github.com/Helithumper/privacy.
